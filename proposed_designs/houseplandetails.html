Design Overview

Refined Living,
Thoughtfully Planned

The Riviera Modern Villa is a study in proportional discipline. Wide horizontal eaves, floor-to-ceiling glazing, and a seamless indoor–outdoor connection define a home that feels both expansive and intimate. Every room has been positioned to maximise natural light and cross-ventilation without sacrificing privacy.

Designed by our principal architect with 22 years of residential experience, this plan is already beloved in over 40 completed builds across Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania — adapting beautifully to both compact urban plots and expansive suburban land.
